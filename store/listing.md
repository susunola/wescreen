## English (en-US)

### Description

<!-- BEGIN DESCRIPTION: en -->
WeScreen records your screen, a window, or a browser tab and saves it as a WebM video — entirely on your own device. No account, no sign-in, no upload, no tracking. There is no WeScreen server, and the extension makes no network requests at all.

Most screen recorders ask you to send your recording somewhere before you can do anything with it. WeScreen does the opposite: the file is written to your disk and nowhere else, so nothing can leak from a server that does not exist.

BUILT FOR RECORDING THAT YOU CONTROL

• 3 or 5 second countdown, so you can switch to the window you want to demonstrate
• Pause and resume at any time without splitting your recording
• Automatic stop after 5, 15, or 30 minutes
• Global keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + S to stop, Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + U to pause or resume, working even when another app has focus
• A custom file name, applied to the downloaded file

AUDIO THAT ACTUALLY MIXES

• Capture system or tab audio and microphone narration together in one track
• Toggle each source independently
• Noise suppression and echo cancellation on the microphone
• If a microphone is unavailable or the shared source carries no audio, WeScreen says so plainly and keeps recording instead of producing a silent file or failing

OUTPUT YOU CAN CHOOSE

• Resolution cap of 1080p or 720p, applied to the captured track — or keep the source resolution untouched
• 30 or 60 frames per second, independently of quality
• Standard, high, or space-saving bitrate presets
• Preview the recording in the browser before you download it

RECORDINGS THAT SURVIVE MISTAKES

• Video chunks are written to local storage every second, so an interrupted session can be recovered when you reopen the recorder
• The recorder shows how much has been captured against the safe limit, and warns before the browser runs out of memory
• Available disk space is displayed, with a warning when it runs low
• Closing the tab in the middle of a recording asks for confirmation first

PRIVATE BY CONSTRUCTION

• Two permissions only: storage (your preferences and the local recovery chunks) and downloads (saving the finished file)
• No host permissions, no access to your history, tabs, bookmarks, or any website content
• No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no remotely hosted code
• What gets captured is decided entirely by the browser's own sharing picker; WeScreen cannot read your screen unless you choose a source

Available in English and Chinese, following your browser language.

If you need MP4 rather than WebM, convert the downloaded file with any video tool — we would rather ship an honest native format than a bundled encoder.
<!-- END DESCRIPTION: en -->

## Chinese (zh-CN)

### Description

<!-- BEGIN DESCRIPTION: zh -->
WeScreen 可以录制你的整个屏幕、单个窗口或浏览器标签页，并保存为 WebM 视频——全部在你自己的设备上完成。不需要账号，不需要登录，没有上传，也没有追踪。WeScreen 没有服务器，扩展本身不发起任何网络请求。

大多数录屏工具要求你先把录像传到某处，才能对它做任何事。WeScreen 反过来：文件只写入你的磁盘，不去别处；不存在的服务器，也就无从泄露。

为"可控的录制过程"而设计

• 3 秒或 5 秒倒计时，方便你切换到要演示的窗口
• 随时暂停与继续，不会把一次录制切成两段
• 5、15 或 30 分钟后自动停止
• 全局快捷键：Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + S 停止，Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + U 暂停或继续，其他应用获得焦点时同样有效
• 自定义文件名，直接应用到下载的文件

真正做了混音的音频

• 把系统/标签页音频与麦克风旁白混合到同一条音轨
• 两个来源可独立开关
• 麦克风启用降噪与回声消除
• 麦克风不可用或共享源没有声音时，WeScreen 会明确说明并继续录制，而不是给你一个没声音的文件，也不会直接失败

可以自己选的输出

• 1080p 或 720p 分辨率上限，应用到采集轨道；也可以完全保持源分辨率
• 30 或 60 帧每秒，与画质互不影响
• 标准、高画质、节省空间三档码率预设
• 下载前先在浏览器内预览

不怕出错的录制

• 视频分片每秒写入本机存储，意外中断后重新打开录制器即可恢复
• 录制中显示已写入的体积与安全上限，在浏览器内存耗尽前给出提示
• 显示本机可用磁盘空间，不足时警告
• 录制中关闭标签页会先请求确认

从设计上就是私密的

• 只申请两项权限：storage（保存你的偏好设置与本地恢复分片）和 downloads（保存录制文件）
• 不申请主机权限，不读取你的历史记录、标签页、书签或任何网站内容
• 没有统计分析、没有遥测、没有广告、不加载任何远程代码
• 录什么完全由浏览器自带的共享选择器决定；除非你主动选择共享源，WeScreen 无法读取你的屏幕

提供中文与英文界面，跟随你的浏览器语言。

如果你需要 MP4 而不是 WebM，可以用任意视频工具转换下载的文件——我们宁愿提供一个诚实可用的原生格式，也不愿塞进一个内置编码器。
<!-- END DESCRIPTION: zh -->

## Certification testing notes

Paste into the **Notes for certification** box at submission.

<!-- BEGIN CERT NOTES -->
WeScreen records the screen, a window, or a browser tab to a local WebM file. No account, network
request, or test credentials are required — the extension is fully local and works offline.

HOW TO TEST

1. Load the extension, then click the WeScreen toolbar icon and choose "Open the recorder".
2. On the recorder page, pick a resolution and set Countdown to "No wait".
3. Click "Start recording". Edge shows its own sharing picker; choose "Entire screen" or any window
   or tab. (With a countdown selected, recording starts 3 seconds after you confirm the source.)
4. The recorder switches to the recording view. The timer counts up, and a size readout next to it
   shows roughly how much has been captured.
5. Click "Pause" — the timer freezes. Click "Resume" — it continues. "Stop recording" ends the take.
6. The result view shows an in-browser preview and a "Download WebM" button. The file is saved to
   your Downloads folder as <name>.webm.

WHAT REVIEWERS SHOULD KNOW

- Screen capture requires user consent through Edge's own picker. The extension cannot capture
  anything the tester does not select; cancelling the picker shows "Screen selection was cancelled."
- Microphone narration is opt-in (unchecked by default). If you enable it and deny the microphone
  permission, recording continues with screen video and system audio, and the recorder states that
  the microphone was unavailable. This is intentional, not an error.
- The toolbar badge shows "REC" while a recording is in progress.
- The global shortcuts Ctrl+Shift+S (stop) and Ctrl+Shift+U (pause/resume) are Edge extension
  commands; they work while another window has focus.
- Recording chunks are stored in IndexedDB during a session so an accidental close can be recovered.
  If the recorder is reopened with chunks left over from an interrupted session, a recovery banner
  offers to download or discard them.
- The extension makes no network requests. It requests only the storage and downloads permissions.
- Displayed dates and language follow the browser locale; the UI ships in English and Chinese.
<!-- END CERT NOTES -->
